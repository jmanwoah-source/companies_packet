## Honest read (what the noise is and isn't)
- The macro de-rating (SMH -2.9%) is the common factor and is REMOVED above. Any residual that survives it is NOT macro drift.
- **Idiosyncratic flags** (|residual| ≥ 20%) are a different noise the sector residual cannot remove: a company-specific event (e.g. a Chapter 11 filing, an unrelated product surge). These are excluded from the substitution read by flagging them, not by ignoring the sector.
- A single shock can only cleanly validate the drift(s) it actually touches (here the 800V/power-rewire drifts). Drifts it doesn't touch show ~0 or noise — that is correct, not a failure.
- **Multi-shock replication** (each shock with the same per-drift residual decomposition) is the path to an underwritable number — only current windows are needed, no gray historical data.
