# QCOM — Edge-AI Silicon Position vs Its Edge-AI TAM

## Thesis at a Glance
- QCOM is the default edge-AI silicon supplier across mobile, industrial, automotive, and (increasingly) defense/edge-inference — the "CPU of the edge tier" with a data-center option via its CPU/server push.

## Financial Snapshot
- QCOM: Q3 FY2026 revenue $9.9B (Qualcomm IR release, July 2026).
- Market cap: near-ceiling flag ($150B policy) — verify exact figure next run.
- Edge-AI revenue split (data-center / auto / handset / industrial): verify next run from 10-Q segment detail.

## Strategic Comparison (single-name deep dive)
- Handset base: the cash engine that funds the edge expansion; cyclically exposed to the handset downcycle.
- Automotive: Snapdragon Ride/Digital Cockpit design-wins = multi-year content per vehicle; the compounding leg.
- Industrial/edge inference: NPU-class SoCs for robotics, defense, and factory-floor autonomy — the smallest but fastest-growing segment.
- Data-center CPU push: the contested tier (Intel/AMD/ARM-server competition) — the swing factor for the multiple.
- Defense adjacency: the physical-AI pillar consumes QCOM edge silicon (see physical-ai-edge-automation.md).

## Edge and Asymmetry
- Convex side: the edge-inference TAM (robotics + defense + factory) is a small % of revenue today and growing faster than the handset base — the mix shift is the story.
- Downside: handset cycle + server-CPU competition are both live risks; the $150B cap means the re-rate is a mix story, not a multiple blow-out.
- Track assignment: near_ceiling flag applies; Track A candidate only if safety metrics + the edge mix-shift both clear.

## Catalysts Ahead
1. Data-center/server CPU design-win announcements (the swing event).
2. Automotive ASP-per-vehicle inflection (content growth).
3. Defense/edge-inference contract awards (the pillar connection).
4. Handset cycle recovery (the cash engine re-accelerating).
