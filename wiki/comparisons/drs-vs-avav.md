## Strategic Comparison
- Stack position: DRS = sensing (radar, RF, sensor fusion) vs AVAV = platforms + effectors (autonomous drones, C-UAS engagement).

- Competition: DRS competes with LHX/NOC radar programs; AVAV competes in the loitering-munition/autonomous-UAS class with a smaller incumbent set.
- Asymmetry of scale: DRS rides Leonardo's global platform (larger, less convex); AVAV is small-cap and concentrated in the new category (smaller, more convex).
