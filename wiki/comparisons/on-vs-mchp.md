## Strategic Comparison
- Product mix: ON is weighted to power (SiC/GaN, VRMs, industrial) — higher beta to the data-center power rewire; MCHP is weighted to breadth (MCUs, interface, timing) — more defensive through the industrial trough.
- Cycle position: MCHP carries the older industrial/automotive inventory-cycle overhang; ON has already repriced the trough.
- 800V rewire exposure: ON is closer to the data-center power story (SiC content per rack); MCHP's exposure is indirect (industrial automation).
- Design-win density: ON wins are power-converter reference designs (concentrated, lumpy); MCHP wins are breadth (many small SKUs, diversified).

## Edge and Asymmetry
- Convex side: ON — every 800V architecture adoption event is direct content upside with no fuel-cost equivalent; the rewire is a one-time content reset.
- Defined side: MCHP — broader base, less volatile, but the re-rate requires the industrial cycle to turn first.
- For Track B: ON is the cleaner data-center power-silicon proxy; MCHP is an industrial-recovery trade, not a pillar play.

## Catalysts Ahead
