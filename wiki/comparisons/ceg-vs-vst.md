# CEG vs VST — Nuclear Fleets Meeting Data-Center Demand

## Thesis at a Glance
- CEG: the largest pure-play US nuclear fleet, deepest hyperscaler PPA book (Microsoft-class deals); the reference asset for zero-carbon baseload.
- VST: diversified PJM nuclear + gas fleet with Meta support agreements; more earnings diversification, less nuclear purity.

## Financial Snapshot
- VST: ~$47.3B market cap at ~$140.83/share (stockanalysis.com, 2026-10-22 close; -9.81% on the day).
- VST: Meta nuclear support agreements announced 2026-01-09 covering PJM nuclear plants + new generation (Vistra IR / PR Newswire).
- CEG: market cap and fleet MWh to verify next run (FMP batch-market-cap at next Deep-Dive pass).
- Both: revenue/margin detail verify next run.

## Strategic Comparison
- Nuclear purity: CEG (fleet is ~all nuclear) vs VST (nuclear + gas + storage mix).
- Hyperscaler contracts: CEG's PPA book is the market benchmark; VST's Meta agreements add a second hyperscaler anchor.
- Price action context: VST -9.81% on 2026-10-22 — volatility is elevated in the nuclear-power complex; position sizing matters more than entry thesis.
- Regulatory/siting: both benefit from PJM capacity-market scarcity pricing; CEG additionally holds restart-optionality (fleet reactivation economics).

## Edge and Asymmetry
- Convex side: CEG — purest expression of the "nuclear is the only zero-carbon baseload" thesis; every new hyperscaler PPA is accretive with no fuel-cost tail.
- VST's diversification is a feature in a rate/price drawdown (gas earnings cushion), but it dilutes the nuclear-specific re-rating.
- For the rubber-band-man setup (Track A): CEG's drawdowns on nuclear-power volatility are the higher-conviction re-entry points.

## Catalysts Ahead
1. Next hyperscaler PPA signature (either name) — the sector's recurring event.
2. PJM capacity auction results (scarcity price resets).
3. VST gas-fleet earnings print (the diversification leg).
4. CEG restart/derate decisions on legacy units.
