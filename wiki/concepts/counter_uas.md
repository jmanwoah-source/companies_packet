# Counter-UAS: Full Detect-AND-Intercept Stack

**Thesis.** Drones are cheap ($200-3k) and mass-producible; the defense budget is being
reallocated from expensive kill vehicles to cheap detection+interception. US Army awarded
**$4.15B** (10 IDIQ counter-drone contracts) in 2026; the full detect-and-kill stack is the
hole the old single-function sensor vendors can't fill.

## Demand
- US Army (LIDS program), DHS (2031 IDIQ), DoD counter-UAS enterprise. 2026 NDAA expanded
  legal authority for state/local law-enforcement drone mitigation = non-military market opening.

## Displacement (the DOOMED contrast)
- **Advantaged:** RTX (Raytheon) — full detect+intercept LIDS stack with named $729M program.
- **DOOMED incumbent:** single-function "soft-kill only" detection vendors (non-kinetic jammers /
  standalone radar) — a detect-only stack loses to a detect-and-kill stack once the buyer wants
  the full chain. Contrast: detection is necessary but no longer sufficient.

## Time-to-fill
- ~18-36 months to saturate federal; gated by 2026 NDAA authority expansion (state/local) and
  the DHS 2031 IDIQ ramp. Directed-energy kill is the 3-5 year frontier (Pentagon 4-vendor pilot).
