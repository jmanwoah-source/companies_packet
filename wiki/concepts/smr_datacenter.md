# SMR / Nuclear Power for AI Data Centers (the 2030 story sold as 2026)

**Thesis.** Hyperscalers have committed 10+ GW of nuclear for AI; Meta locked **6.6 GW**
(Vistra/TerraPower/Oklo) for its Ohio Prometheus cluster. But most of the signed GW is EXISTING
reactor uprates, not SMRs — the gap is who actually delivers NEW SMR capacity by the 2030
deadline they are already pricing as 2026. That delivery gap is the opportunity.

## Demand
- Meta (6.6 GW, Prometheus/Ohio), Constellation $4.3B uprate deal, Amazon $20B PA (adjacent to
  Susquehanna nuclear plant), Google 20-yr nuclear deal. 45 GW of IEA-counted conditional SMR DC agreements.

## Displacement (the DOOMED contrast)
- **Advantaged:** CEG/TLN — OWN the existing reactors the hyperscalers are actually plugging into
  (near-term revenue, not a 2030 bet).
- **DOOMED incumbent:** the grid-tied utility load forecast (the old base-load model) AND the
  pure-new-SMR vendors who can't deliver by the deadline they are priced for. Contrast: the money
  is in existing capacity today; the SMR story is the 2030 optionality that can underperform.

## Time-to-fill
- Real SMR delivery = 2030 (NRC license + fab). The gap between the 45 GW signed and the sliver
  actually under construction IS the story — most is uprated existing reactors.
