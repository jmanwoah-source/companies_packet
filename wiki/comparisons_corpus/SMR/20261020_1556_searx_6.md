In September, UBS cut NuScale Power stock to Sell over long commercialization timelines, weak customer commitments and heavy expected cash burn. Both calls highlight the same gap, since NuScale Power still generates little commercial revenue while its reactor design awaits a firm order. Story Continues Three Different Paths to Nuclear Revenue NuScale Power licenses and sells small modular reactor designs and modules, so its revenue depends on customers committing to build plants. Its key asset is the only U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission design certification in the small modular reactor industry. That credential is a real edge, though it hasn't yet produced the signed buyer the model needs. Oklo plans to build, own and operate reactors and sell the power they produce. Centrus Energy already earns revenue selling enriched uranium fuel, giving it an established operating business. By comparison, NuScale Power's model leans harder on landing a signed reactor sale. Weighing the Bull and Bear Cases NuScale Power's bullish argument rests on its regulatory head start and its link to a large U.S. program. ENTRA1 Energy, the company's exclusive global strategic partner, is advancing discussions with the Tennessee Valley Authority toward a definitive power purchase agreement for potentially the largest nuclear power deployment program in U.S. history. That program is a major potential source of the binding order NuScale Power still lacks, and it's part of the broader restart trade we mapped across utilities and fuel suppliers in a free nuclear guide. On the bearish side, time and money are the problem. A pre-commercial reactor developer can spend heavily for years before revenue arrives, and NuScale Power stock has already fallen to a new low during that wait. Until a firm order exists, the bearish case keeps its holds. A Binding Contract Is the Next Test NuScale Power's next challenge is whether it signs a binding customer contract. Such an order is the missing piece behind the timeline concerns in the recent downgrades. TVA program updates and upcoming earnings are the catalysts to watch. Until then, SMR shareholders may want to keep their positions small and lean cautious, since a fresh low and a below-market target leave room for more downside.
