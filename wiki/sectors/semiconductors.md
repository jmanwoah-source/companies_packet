# Semiconductors & Subsystems

Pure-play designers, analog/mixed-signal foundries, materials, lithography & fab tools, advanced packaging, and silicon photonics. The quant lens here: the value chain is a *stack of leverage* — a $1B equipment order (KLAC) flows through as a design-win at a fabless, which flows through as a packaging substrate win downstream. Small/mid caps sit at the leverage nodes, not the fabless center of gravity.

## Value Chain Map (upstream → downstream)

**1. Fab / lithography tools (the bottleneck tier).** Photoresist, deposition, etch, CMP, metrology, and the EUV/DUV steppers. Tool spend is the leading indicator of every downstream node.
- ASML (EUV/DUV steppers), AMAT (deposition/etch), KLAC (cleaning/metrology/etch), LRCX (etch/deposition).

**2. Foundry / manufacturing.** Pure-play chip designers contract out silicon here; capacity and yield set the ceiling on the whole pillar.
- TSM, UMC. (OSAT/packaging layer sits just downstream: AMKR, and test/metrology via KLAC/LRCX.)

**3. Analog / mixed-signal & power semis (fabless, high moat).** High-design-win density; content-per-AI-server and content-per-power-rack both rising. The 800V rewire is the live catalyst — see [sic_gan_power](../concepts/sic_gan_power.md).
- ADI, MCHP, NXPI, ON, TXN, MPWR, NVTS, SW (onwideband/GaN), QRVO, WOLF, LSCC.

**4. Materials & substrates (the "boring" upstream with real moats).** Silicon carbide wafers, SOI, optical substrates, packaging materials.
- AXTI, WOLF (SiC/GaN wafer feedstock), TER (substrates/test).

**5. Advanced packaging (2.5D/3D, CoWoS-adjacent).** Where AI die stacks physically come together; the constraint tier as nodes shrink.
- PLAB, AMKR, TER; substrate/test support from KLAC/LRCX.

**6. Silicon photonics / optical I/O.** Co-packaged optics and transceivers move from inter-chip to intra-rack; the new frontier of the value chain.
- COHR, LITE, IIVI, PLAB, AXTI (phosphide/GaAs materials).

## Sub-Sectors and Moats

- **Lithography/fab tools** — deepest moat in the industry (multi-year customer qualification, ~90%+ gross margins on steppers). Only ASML is a credible EUV player; AMAT/KLAC/LRCX duopolize most process steps.
- **Analog/mixed-signal** — moat is *part count + qualification*, not fabs: ADI/TXN/MCHP have decades of automotive/aerospace-qualified parts that take years to reverse-engineer. Fabless = asset-light leverage.
- **Wide-bandgap (SiC/GaN) power** — moat is wafer yield + design-wins. NVTS's 24kW NVIDIA 800V board and ON's EliteSiC modules are the named wins; WOLF supplies the substrate feedstock. See [sic_gan_power](../concepts/sic_gan_power.md).
- **Advanced packaging** — moat is process IP + thermal management; co-packaged optics (PLAB/COHR) compresses the optical-to-die distance.
- **Silicon photonics** — moat is low; this is the *fastest-moving, lowest-moat* tier → highest small-cap asymmetric upside and highest drawdown risk.

## Key Public Companies (nano-to-$150B focus)

- **> $50B (anchor, not the asymmetry):** NVDA, AMD, AVGO, TSM, ASML.
- **$10–50B (design-win density):** ADI, MRVL, TXN, ON, NXPI, AMAT, KLAC, LRCX, COHR, LITE.
- **$1–10B (the quant sweet spot):** MCHP, MPWR, NVTS, QRVO, SWKS, AXTI, PLAB, TER, WOLF, LSCC, AMKR, UMC.
- **Nano / micro (highest beta, verify cash):** SW, IIVI, SMCI-adjacent test plays.

## Secular Catalysts (structural, multi-year)

1. **AI accelerator content per server** keeps compounding — HBM, packaging, optical I/O all ride it.
2. **The 800V data-center power rewire** replaces silicon final-stage with GaN/SiC — a named substitution event (NVTS, ON, WOLF). [sic_gan_power](../concepts/sic_gan_power.md)
3. **Advanced packaging as the new scaling axis** — die-stacking outruns node scaling; co-packaged optics is next.
4. **Geographic fab diversification** (US/TSMC/UMC expansion) re-opens a multi-year equipment cycle.
5. **SiC/GaN substrate supply** — wafer yield is the constraint gating the whole wide-bandgap wave (WOLF, AXTI).
