# Physical AI & Edge Automation

Tactical counter-UAS (C-UAS) hardware, autonomous defense sensors, industrial robotics, and advanced automation components. The structural thesis: the UAS war created a permanent, funded demand category (C-UAS) that did not exist as a budget line in 2021, and edge AI is moving from the cloud into the sensor/actuator tier.

## Value Chain Map (upstream to downstream)

**1. Sensors & perception (upstream).** Radar, EO/IR, RF detection, autonomous target recognition. Sensor fusion + on-board inference is the new design center.
- DRS (counter-drone radar, C-UAS awards), LHX (sensors/missile subsystems), NOC (autonomous targeting), RTX (sensors/autonomy programs).

**2. C-UAS & autonomous effectors.** Detection-to-engagement stacks: directed energy, interceptor drones, RF jamming, autonomous loitering munitions.
- AVAV (autonomous UAS + C-UAS systems), RCAT (counter-UAS), BAESF (radar/engagement).

**3. Edge AI silicon & integration.** On-device inference: NPU-class SoCs, vision processors, low-power edge accelerators.
- QCOM (edge-AI silicon across defense/industrial), NVDA (Jetson-class edge platform demand).

**4. Industrial robotics & automation.** Collaborative arms, AMR fleets, factory automation, surgical/medical robotics.
- ISRG (surgical robotics — largest cap in pillar), AITX (autonomy), TER-class (verify next run).

**5. Defense primes (integration tier).** Program-level integration of the above into weapon systems; the budget anchor.
- LMT, NOC, RTX, HON (aerospace autonomy), ROK (industrial automation).

## Sub-Sectors and Moats

- **C-UAS**: funded category (DoD + allied NATO budgets), low incumbent concentration; program wins = multi-year revenue.
- **Autonomous sensors**: classification + program lock-in; once on a platform, switching costs are extreme.
- **Edge AI silicon**: qualification + reference-design moats; the defense variant of the data-center edge story.
- **Industrial robotics**: install-base + service revenue; compounding through the labor-shortage cycle.

## Key Public Companies (nano to $150B)

- AVAV, DRS, RCAT, BAESF — C-UAS/autonomous UAS (small-to-mid cap; highest asymmetric optionality in the pillar).
- LHX, NOC, RTX, LMT — sensor/autonomy primes (large cap; near-ceiling flag on LMT/NOC/RTX).
- QCOM — edge-AI silicon (large cap; near-ceiling flag).
- ISRG — surgical robotics (large cap; near-ceiling flag).
- AITX, HON, ROK — autonomy/industrial automation.

## Secular Catalysts

1. C-UAS budget lines becoming permanent (multi-year, not surge-funded).
2. NATO/Allied C-UAS procurement programs awarding (first contract awards = the catalyst).
3. On-board inference standardizing (autonomous target recognition without human-in-loop).
4. Industrial robotics labor-shortage compounding (install base → service revenue flywheel).
