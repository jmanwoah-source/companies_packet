# Advanced Energy & Grid

Next-generation nuclear, small modular reactors (SMR), high-capacity grid distribution, the uranium/fuel cycle, and data-center power generation. The structural thesis: data-center demand growth (multi-year) outruns interconnection queue capacity, and nuclear is the only zero-carbon base-load source that clears siting and permitting constraints at scale.

## Value Chain Map (upstream to downstream)

**1. Fuel cycle (upstream).** Enriched uranium supply, fuel fabrication, and the long-lead reprocessing/oxide chain. Concentration is rising with reactor restarts (Three Mile Island, Palisades-class restarts).
- WULF (uranium production), SUI (uranium exploration/production).

**2. Reactor technology & SMR developers.** The optionality tier: licensing (NRC standardization) is the moat and the gate.
- OKLO (Aurora microreactor), NUS (NuScale SMR), BWXT (fission core components + microreactor fuel), HTZ (Holtec — reactor refurbishment/SMR), MOD (Modular — fission power).
- Cross-link: [smr_datacenter](../concepts/smr_datacenter.md).

**3. Nuclear operators & data-center power developers.** The cash-flow tier that actually signs the 20-40 year PPAs hyperscalers are writing.
- CEG (largest US nuclear fleet; hyperscaler PPAs), TLN (Talen — nuclear + data-center adjacency), VST (Vistra — PJM nuclear + Meta agreements), GEV (GE Vernova — T&D + turbine + nuclear services).

**4. Grid distribution & power equipment.** The bottleneck tier: transformers, switchgear, HVDC, grid software. Lead times (transformers 3-5 years) make this the binding constraint.
- GEV (transformers/turbines), ETN-class industrial electrical (verify dossier next run).

**5. Data-center power generation (downstream customers).** Hyperscaler PPAs, behind-the-meter nuclear, on-site gas.
- SMCI (server/rack adjacency), VRT (thermal/power delivery into the rack).

## Sub-Sectors and Moats

- **Nuclear fleet ownership**: permit + operating-license moats; 40-year asset lives; zero-fuel-variance margins.
- **SMR licensing**: NRC standard design approval = decade-long head start; the moat is regulatory, not capital.
- **T&D equipment**: 3-5 year transformer lead times + aging workforce = pricing power through the cycle.
- **Uranium supply**: concentrated, long-lead, sanction-sensitive; restarts re-open the bid for enriched uranium.

## Key Public Companies (nano to $150B)

- CEG, VST, TLN — operators with signed hyperscaler demand (largest caps in pillar).
- GEV — grid equipment + nuclear services (near-ceiling cap; flagged).
- OKLO, NUS, BWXT, HTZ, MOD — SMR/technology developers (nano-to-small cap; binary licensing catalysts).
- WULF, SUI — fuel-cycle exposure.

## Secular Catalysts

1. Hyperscaler PPAs converting to construction (CEG/Microsoft-class deals, VST/Meta).
2. NRC standardization of SMR designs cutting licensing timelines.
3. Transformer/HVDC lead-time inflation repricing the grid-equipment tier.
4. Uranium restart bids re-opening fuel-cycle pricing.
