## Additive Assessment
vGaN targets the power delivery chain (grid-to-chip), displacing silicon at lower voltage (<600V) and SiC at higher voltage (>600V). This is additive to the broader GaN ecosystem (lateral GaN for consumer charging, etc.) while creating displacement risk for incumbents entrenched in silicon and SiC platforms.
