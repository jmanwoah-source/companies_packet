## Additive Assessment
Mostly additive. Copper AECs will persist in scale-out and datacenter interconnect (DCI) layers. CPO displacement concentrates on scale-up and scale-in (intra-rack, intra-package) where optical interconnects move from the module level directly to the package level. The shift is additive to total optical content while displacing the most power-intensive copper links.
